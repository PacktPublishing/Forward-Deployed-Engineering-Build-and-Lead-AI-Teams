## Scaling Triggers

- **Trigger:** Any role exceeds 85 percent utilization for two planning cycles **Action:** Open capacity plan and reduce uncommitted scope
- **Trigger:** A shared specialist becomes the sole qualified owner for two or more critical engagements **Action:** Assign a shadow and create a dated capability transfer plan
- **Trigger:** Security or architecture review wait exceeds 10 business days **Action:** Add reviewer capacity or narrow concurrent high-risk work
- **Trigger:** More than two engagements enter go-live or hypercare together **Action:** Create a temporary command-center rotation and executive priority review
- **Trigger:** Reusable work exceeds 30 percent of delivery effort **Action:** Fund a dedicated product or platform workstream

## Anti-Hero Controls

- Primary and backup DRI on every engagement
- Decision and runbook artifacts stored in shared repositories
- Observed handoffs before role rotation
- Shared on-call and review rotations
- No critical approval dependent on one person

## Governance

- Weekly portfolio staffing review — FDE Director: Priority changes, Specialist conflicts, Hiring or contractor trigger
- Weekly architecture and security council — Principal FDE: Design exceptions, Security risk acceptance, Reusable pattern approval
- Biweekly talent review — FDE Manager: Coaching load, Backup coverage, Career evidence and rotations
